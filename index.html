Key Features

Less re-authoring.
More teaching.

SlideSync ingests your existing decks, extracts topics with RAG-grounded research, and surfaces updates with confidence scoring — so you stay in control.

AI Topic Extraction

Upload PDF, PPT or PPTX and SlideSync's RAG pipeline identifies the main topics and key concepts in each slide.

Confidence-Based Updates

High-confidence changes auto-apply. Low-confidence ones surface as suggestions you can review, approve, or reject.

Side-by-Side E-Slides

Compare original slides with AI-generated update notes inline. Edit keywords directly, then export the enhanced deck.

RAG-Powered Sources

Every suggestion is tied back to the external resources behind it — peer-reviewed papers, docs, and trusted publications.

Tunable Recency

Set timeframes, preferred sources, exclusions, and the kinds of updates you want. SlideSync respects your editorial voice.

Export Anywhere

Download enhanced decks as PDF or PPTX. Share securely across your institution with role-based access controls.

For Instructors

Built for academic workflows

Three steps from a stale slide deck to a current, citation-backed lecture.

01

Upload your deck

Drag in PDF, PPT, or PPTX files. We validate and extract every slide, preserving structure and ordering.

02

Set your preferences

Choose recency, preferred and excluded sources, summarization style, and the types of updates you want.

03

Review and export

Approve AI suggestions in the side-by-side workspace, then export the enhanced deck back to your LMS.
